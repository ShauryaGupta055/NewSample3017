    Platform overview

    Every record scanned, read, checked and mapped

    Open any stage to see what it covers.

    Each extracted field is scored for confidence. Anything below threshold is queued for a human reviewer rather than written silently into the archive.

    • Confidence scoring
    • Gap detection
    • Review queue

    Pages are deskewed and cleaned before reading, so faded registers and stamped margins still yield usable text and structured metadata.

    • Deskew & clean
    • Field metadata
    • Regional scripts

    Reviewers compare the digital entry against the scanned page side by side, and every correction is attributed and logged.

    • Side-by-side check
    • Attributed edits
    • Audit log

    Verified parcels are tied to survey numbers and plotted, so a holding can be found by owner, survey number or village boundary.

    • Parcel geometry
    • Survey linkage
    • Search by owner
